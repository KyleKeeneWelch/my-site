Summary

This was the first project I partook in after joining Feral Interactive. The project was halfway during development so it required getting to grips with new skills and getting familiar with the new game. Empire Total War required UX solutions to tackle turn-based, faction management systems that meant that the player needed to be informed of a variety of information across several screens all on a smaller device. Key challenges involved:

  • Arranging Information - Designing panels that were easy to understand, access, and catered for a specific purpose. Information on your relationship with other factions could easily be seen in the Diplomacy Panel for example and accessed straight from a button in the Campaign UI.
  • Localised Text - Supporting a range of languages and having the UI handle the extra characters needed for some languages. Smart placement, allocation of space, and text scrolling assisted with this.
  • Optimising for Mobile and Tablet - Creating responsive layouts that work better on wider aspect ratios and taller, more square ratios to accommodate a range of mobile and tablet devices.
  • Tooltips - Extra information provided in the form of tooltips when interacting with certain UI or in-world elements. Tooltips were given a dedicated, expandable space that the player could easily refer to when they needed additional information to reinforce a decision.
