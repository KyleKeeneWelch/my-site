Summary

I had spent some of my time working on this project and the rest of my time divided between other projects leading up to its initial release. It meant that I was familiar enough to lead the Design of the Contracts mode patch which released on May 14th 2026. Many of the challenges I faced were related to the Contracts patch and the mode added after initial release. Key challenges involved:

  • Leaderboards and Competitions - Alongside contracts were contract leaderboards as well as leaderboard categories to group the Top 100 players amongst various statistics. Players could also create private competitions between Friends that challenge them to beat their score on a contract they have created or liked. Developing UI and systems to appropriately manage scores and entice players to play again was a difficult game loop to implement, especially since the original mode handled this well. Formated tables and the messages feature was used to overcome this.
  • Messages - The original game used a messages system to deal with the social aspect of the mode which was not something we had planned for the port's patch. Instead, the system was repurposed as a notification system to notify players of featured contracts and changes to their competitions / competitions they were part of.
  • Unlocking Maps and Targets - The original mode did not offer all the maps and targets for players to use to create their contracts. Unlocking these and catering for the new methods to eliminate was a tricky but worthy addition to the patch.
  • Localised Text - Supporting a range of languages and having the UI handle the extra characters needed for some languages. Smart placement, allocation of space, and text scrolling assisted with this.
  • Optimising for Mobile and Tablet - Creating responsive layouts that work better on wider aspect ratios and taller, more square ratios to accommodate a range of mobile and tablet devices.
