Summary

Another project playing a supportive UX role however this was a big step up from the Isometric Puzzler that was Guardian of Light. Employed new practices that boosted productivity and maintainability that are in use today. Key challenges involved:

  • Touch Controls and conflicting placement - Designing a touch interface that is intuitive and works alongside the original is hard enough without needing it to work across various mobile and tablet screen sizes. Understanding the game and what is needed by the player at what point was key in toggling what controls are shown and where so that it feels intuitive and natural.
  • QTE - Quick time events and other quick interactions required lots of thought and iteration to find a middle ground in making moments feel tense but not impossible to deal with. These were brought across as time based taps instead of difficult mashes or events that would be unsuitable for touch.
  • Localised Text - Supporting a range of languages and having the UI handle the extra characters needed for some languages. Smart placement, allocation of space, and text scrolling assisted with this.
  • Optimising for Mobile and Tablet - Creating responsive layouts that work better on wider aspect ratios and taller, more square ratios to accommodate a range of mobile and tablet devices.
