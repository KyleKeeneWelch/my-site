Summary

Played a supportive role in the UX development of this project. First dive into something that wasn't strategy and had new challenges. One of which was the co-op, multiplayer functionality. This required additional UX to cater for the need of setting up an account, sending invites, and joining sessions. Key challenges involved:

  • Development of Agnostic Screens - To support co-op multiplayer there was a need to flesh out the Feral agnostic screens as well as the in-game lobby to be able to handle sessions with multiple players.
  • Localised Text - Supporting a range of languages and having the UI handle the extra characters needed for some languages. Smart placement, allocation of space, and text scrolling assisted with this.
  • Optimising for Mobile and Tablet - Creating responsive layouts that work better on wider aspect ratios and taller, more square ratios to accommodate a range of mobile and tablet devices.
